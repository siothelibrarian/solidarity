# Mutual Aid

MAPE members have created a mutual aid group to provide support and resources to our members, siblings in other unions, and communities. 

You can find more information about how to get connected with us via email, Signal, or Discord in our [Intro to MAPE Mutual Aid Coalition](https://docs.proton.me/u/2/doc?mode=open-url&token=AEFCRDE63W&linkId=Q-6jmCnLZSSNggp14Q6LCA_zJSO4nSzx9YgRMWwSkOPpxTcF3A1iqyZNU9iRD2O1lXmyfzN7VPBN1ZfnUo1BaA%3D%3D#hrFaRkxr3Jd7) document.

## Need help?
You can find a spreadsheet of resources that we have compiled here: [MAPE Mutual Aid Skill Share Ideas](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1-CSFptVe2PldWY5BKzxRCqjjAp042gy_O5bqtmysOjg/edit?gid=0#gid=0)
It includes:
- Food support resources in the Twin Cities and Greater MN
- Housing and rapid response resources
- A list of skills and workshop ideas that folks have proposed or offered.

If you need support with a work-related issue, you can find a steward for your region/local on the MAPE website: https://mape.org/locals/find-your-steward
Keep in mind that stewards are folks with full-time jobs and lives just like you - so if you don't hear back, try a different person! It's usually best to start with one of more of the Regional Steward Directors, since they manage the other stewards and will know who is a good fit to help with your problem.

You can also get advice and support on the MAPE Discord server (MAPE-repped folks only). We have discussions about benefits, working conditions, upcoming and ongoing labor actions, and a bunch of other stuff. Plus - adorable pictures of MAPE pets! If you're not already on the server, you can email Sio (SioInSolidarity@proton.me) and she'll get you an invite.
